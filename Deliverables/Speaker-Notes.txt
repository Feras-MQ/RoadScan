SLIDE 1 — 20 seconds
0:20 — RoadScan helps turn a road image into a reviewable damage report. Our contribution is the complete inspection workflow: recognition, correction, repair-priority support and controlled preparation of training data. It is a capstone prototype, not structural safety certification.
Sources: RoadScan-Project-Report.pdf sections 1–2; OLO AIEP brief, Track A. Artwork is the current app’s illustrative road scene, not an AI prediction. Team roles are not individually assigned because contribution records have not been verified.

SLIDE 2 — 35 seconds
0:35 — The browser accepts a photo, camera stream or video. FastAPI validates the input and runs the selected local detector. Predictions become saved reports for human review; SQLAlchemy and SQLite retain decisions while images and model files remain in local storage. Priority and optional nearby matching support the reviewer. Only approved examples enter the export workflow.
Sources: RoadScan-Project-Report.pdf architecture section; ../roadscan/api.py, core.py and frontend/src/App.tsx. The arrows describe the user workflow; matching is optional and training is a separate process.

SLIDE 3 — 50 seconds
0:50 — This is an actual current demo report. The reviewer can inspect and correct boxes, record a note and choose a status. An optional vote compares separate model outputs, but a majority is not ground truth: the reviewer can use an individual model’s draft and then save the decision. Demo or synthetic records are demonstrations, not evidence of local-road accuracy.
Sources: Evidence/Design-2026-10-27/deployed-review-desktop.png; ../docs/LIVE_AND_MODELS.md; frontend/src/ModelVote.tsx and review workflow. If showing the app live, keep the original demo report unchanged or use a disposable report.

SLIDE 5 — 40 seconds
0:40 — Camera mode starts analysis automatically after recognition is started, with one inference in flight at a time. It is responsive sampling rather than a promise to process every arriving camera frame. For stored video, the separate complete-frame job processes decoded frames sequentially and reports progress; it can run slower than the recording. The active model and input size affect speed. Iriun can act as a webcam, as tested by the team, but this deck makes no representative live FPS claim.
Sources: ../docs/LIVE_AND_MODELS.md; frontend/src/LiveRecognition.tsx and FullVideoAnalysis.tsx; current 27 October screenshot. Direct drone telemetry/control is not implemented; compatible footage can be submitted for experimental analysis.

SLIDE 6 — 45 seconds
0:45 — Learning is an explicit workflow. Correct and save the report, approve the example, then export the dataset. A training tool trains a separate checkpoint. Evaluate on protected unused data before importing it and selecting Use model. Review does not silently change the deployed model, and training gains are not guaranteed. This makes each model change deliberate and reversible.
Sources: current Learning UI screenshot, 27 October; Quick-Start.md and ../docs/LIVE_AND_MODELS.md; training export/approval code. Keep validation and test data isolated from approved training examples.
